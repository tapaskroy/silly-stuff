Method, parameters and sources
  • Polling average. Same procedure as the Senate and House sheets: every poll since Aug 1 counted once, weighted by recency (half-life 14 days), √sample size, LV/RV, ×0.5 for sponsored polls, ×0.85 if seen only in a secondary table; sponsored polls shifted 4 pts against their sponsor.
  • Fundamentals prior. Governors run far ahead of or behind their party, so this prior is deliberately weak (SD 11; 14 with no polls). It blends the 2024 presidential margin (state lean × 0.75, plus 0.55 × the +10.2 national swing, plus 8 pts for an incumbent) with the last governor result adjusted for the swing since (20% weight).
  • Correlated error. National SD 3, regional 1.25, state 3.75 + sampling; Student-t, 6 df. No partisan bias assumed in the baseline.
  • Ranked choice applies only in Alaska; Maine uses plurality for governor. Pollsters' final-round numbers are used where published. Georgia requires a majority (Dec 1 runoff), not modeled since it is a two-candidate race.
  • Tiers: Safe ≥ 99%, Likely ≥ 90%, Lean ≥ 75%, Tilt ≥ 60%, else Toss-up. The 6-point rule only flags deep dives (the original sheet used 10).
  • national_sd: SD of cycle-level governor poll bias 3.1 (3.4 since 2010); governor and Senate cycle biases correlate 0.88 (538 raw_polls recompute 1998-2024)
  • regional_sd: Census-region clustering near zero (implied ~0.5); 1.25 kept to cover demographic misses like 2020
  • state_sd: Residual race error after removing cycle bias: 4.05 for competitive races with 4+ polls, 3.7 with 11+
  • single_poll_sd: Individual governor polls scatter 4.43 around their race mean
  • per_undecided: Absolute error rises ~0.09 per undecided point (r = 0.20)
  • fundamentals_sd: Fitted presidential-lean + swing residual in competitive races since 2012: 9.2-10.3; 11 used
  • fundamentals_sd_nopoll: All races: 13.2-14.0, slightly fat-tailed
  • pres_lean_coef: Presidential-margin coefficient for governor margins 0.73-0.83 in competitive races since 2012 (vs ~0.8-1 for Senate)
  • national_shift_factor: Fitted national-swing coefficient for governors 0.37-0.62, vs 0.66-0.80 for Senate
  • incumbency_bonus: Regression estimates 10-12 (8 in competitive races), shaded for selection bias (weak incumbents retire)
  • last_gov_weight: Adding the previous governor margin barely improves the fit (SD 14.0 -> 13.8), so it gets 20%
  • partisan_shift: Sponsored governor polls lean 3.9-4.3 toward the sponsor (n = 137-244)
  • lean_shift: Judgment: unsponsored one-party pollsters, roughly half the sponsored effect
  • prior_bias: Zero: governor poll bias has leaned D on average (+1.1 to +2.1) but its sign does not persist (lag-1 r ~ 0); tested as scenarios
  • t_df: State errors mildly fat-tailed (implied df ~13); 6 used because of 2020's +8.3 national miss
  • independent_extra_sd: Judgment: thin record for independent-vs-R races
