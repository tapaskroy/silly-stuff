Reading the vote shares. A state's vote share counts every House race in it, so races without a candidate from one party pull that party's share down: California's top-two system puts two Democrats (or two Republicans) on some ballots, and unopposed incumbents get every vote, or none at all where the state doesn't count unopposed races. States marked * have such races (hover the mark, or see the list below). Seat shares leave out uncalled seats. The counts are AP's and will move a little as states certify.
