2026 Midterm Forecasts

Probabilistic forecasts for the Senate, House and governor races on Nov 3, 2026, built from individual polls and a documented fundamentals model with correlated polling error, and no race-by-race judgment calls. Each sheet has levers to try your own scenario: a polling miss, more or less uncertainty, and calling the too-close-to-call races yourself. Data as of 2026-11-03.

The headline numbers assume the polls are right on average. Each card also shows the same simulation with every race shifted 2 or 3 points toward Republicans (R+2, R+3), the size of polling miss seen in several recent elections. For the House the shift comes on top of the 1-point shading toward Republicans already in the forecast.
